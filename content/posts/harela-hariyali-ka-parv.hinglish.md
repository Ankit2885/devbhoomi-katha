Barsaat ki pehli phuhaarein, kheton mein nayi jutai ki taiyari aur ghar ke mandir ke paas mitti ki tokri mein uge hare-peele ankur. Yahi hai Uttarakhand ka apna parv **Harela** — yaani **"hariyali ka din"**.

## Harela kab manaya jaata hai?

Harela **Shravan maas ke pehle din** manaya jaata hai, jise **Kark Sankranti** ya **Shravan Sankranti** bhi kehte hain. Yeh aamtaur par **July ke madhya** mein padta hai. Yeh din **barsaat ki shuruaat** aur **buvaai ke naye chakra** ka prateek hai, isliye kheti par nirbhar pahadi parivaron ke liye yeh bahut shubh maana jaata hai.

## Alag-alag naam

- **Kumaon** mein yeh parv sabse lokpriya hai aur yahin ise **Harela** kehte hain.
- **Garhwal** ke kai hisson mein ise **Mol Sankranti** ya **Rai-Sagran** ke naam se jaana jaata hai.
- Himachal Pradesh ke kuch kshetron mein yahi parv **Hariyali/Rihyali** ya **Dakhrain** kehlata hai.

## Harela kaise ugaya jaata hai?

Tyohar se **das din pehle** ghar ke bade sadasya ek tokri ya bartan mein mitti bharkar **5 ya 7 tarah ke anaaj** ke beej bote hain — jaise gehun, jau, makka, urad, sarson aadi. Roz un par paani chhidka jaata hai.

Das din mein ye beej hare-peele ankur ban jaate hain. Inhi ankuron ko **Harela** kehte hain.

## Tyohar ke din kya hota hai?

- Harele ko kaatkar pehle **Kuldevta** aur ghar ke mandir mein chadhaya jaata hai.
- Phir ghar ke bade-buzurg sabke **sir par Harela rakhte hain** — sir se pair tak chhuaate hue aashirwad dete hain.
- Aashirwad ke lok bol kuch aise shuru hote hain:

> "Ji raya, jagi raya…"

Yaani — jeete raho, jaagte raho, phalo-phoolo.

- Nayi fasal ke daanon ko bhoonkar khaya jaata hai, ghar mein **urad ke pakode** aur pakwaan bante hain.
- Rishtedaar ek-doosre se milte hain, aur kai jagah **mele (Kauthig)** lagte hain.

## Harela aur paryavaran

Harele ke din log **naye paudhe lagane** ka sankalp lete hain. Aajkal Uttarakhand ke schoolon mein bhi bachchon ko is din ghar, school ya gaon mein paudhe lagane ke liye prerit kiya jaata hai. Isiliye Harele ko aksar **"Paryavaran Bachao"** ke sandesh se joda jaata hai.

## Saal mein ek nahi, teen Harele

Kumaon mein Harela sirf Saawan mein hi nahi, balki **Chaitra Navratri** aur **Shardiya (Ashwin) Navratri** ke samay bhi boya jaata hai. Saawan wala Harela sabse bada maana jaata hai.

## Bachchon ki masti

Tyohar se pehle bachche khel-khel mein **gudde-gudiya ka byaah** rachate hain aur baans ki lambi lakdiyon par chadhkar chalne wala khel **Gedi** khelte hain.
