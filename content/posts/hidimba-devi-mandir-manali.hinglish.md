Manali ki bheed-bhaad se thoda upar, oonche-oonche devdar ke pedon ke beech ek anokha mandir hai. Lakdi ki teen chaukor chhatein, unke upar peetal ka shanku aur nakkashi se bhare darwaze. Yeh hai **Hidimba Devi Mandir**, jise sthaniya log **Dhungri Mandir** bhi kehte hain.

## Kaun thin Hidimba?

Mahabharat ke anusaar vanvaas ke dauraan **Pandav** is kshetra mein aaye. Yahan **Hidimb** naam ka ek shaktishaali rakshas rehta tha, jisne Pandavon par hamla kiya. **Bheem** ne yuddh mein use hara diya.

Hidimb ki behen **Hidimba** ne pran liya tha ki woh usi se vivah karegi jo uske bhai ko harayega. Is tarah Hidimba aur Bheem ka vivah hua aur unke putra **Ghatotkach** ka janm hua — wahi Ghatotkach jisne aage chalkar Mahabharat ke yuddh mein badi veerta dikhayi.

## Rakshasi se Devi tak

Jab Pandav vanvaas poora karke laut gaye, to Hidimba unke saath nahi gayin. Ve yahin rehkar **tapasya** karti rahin. Manyata hai ki isi tapasya ke kaaran unhe **Devi ka sthaan** mila. Aaj Kullu-Manali kshetra mein **Hidimba Devi** ko bahut samman se pooja jaata hai.

## Mandir ki khaas baatein

- Mandir **1553 isvi** mein **Maharaja Bahadur Singh** ne banwaya tha.
- Yeh ek **gufa** ke chaaron or bana hai, jahan manyata hai ki Hidimba dhyan karti thin.
- Garbhgriha mein zameen se nikli ek **badi chattan** hai, jise Devi ke roop mein pooja jaata tha. Patthar par bane **Devi ke charan-chinh** ki bhi pooja hoti hai.
- Mandir ka lakdi ka **shikhar lagbhag 24 metre** ooncha hai — teen chaukor chhatein aur sabse upar peetal ki shanku jaisi chhat.
- Mukhya darwaze par Devi Durga, pashu-pakshi, nartak, Krishna-leela aur Navgrahon ki nakkashi hai.
- Mandir se kareeb **70 metre** door **Ghatotkach** ka bhi sthaan hai.
- Chaaron or ka devdar ka jungle **Dhungri Van Vihar** kehlata hai.

## Kab jaayein?

- **Navratri** mein yahan bahut bheed hoti hai — Manali mein in dinon Hidimba Devi ki vishesh pooja hoti hai.
- Har saal **vasant** ke aaspaas **Hidimba Devi Mela** lagta hai.

> Manali jaayein to Mall Road se pehle Dhungri ke devdaron ke beech Maa Hidimba ke darshan zaroor karein.
