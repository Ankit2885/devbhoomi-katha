Himalaya mein **Trishul** aur **Nanda Ghunti** chotiyon ke beech, bahut oonchai par ek chhoti-si jheel hai — **Roopkund**. Saal ke zyadatar mahine yeh barf se jami rehti hai. Lekin jab garmiyon mein barf pighalti hai, to paani ke kinare aur tal par **insaani kankal** dikhai dene lagte hain. Isiliye ise **"Kankal Jheel"** ya **"Rahasya Jheel"** bhi kaha jaata hai.

## Kankal kab mile?

1942 mein Nanda Devi kshetra ke ek van ranger **Hari Kishan Madhwal** ne yahan kankal dekhe. Us samay doosra vishwa yuddh chal raha tha, isliye Angrez afsaron ko pehle darr hua ki ye kahin chhipkar aaye Japani sainik to nahi. Lekin jaanch mein pata chala ki kankal usse kahin zyada purane hain.

Yahan ab tak **300 se zyada** logon ke avshesh mil chuke hain. Saath mein lakdi ki cheezein, lohe ke bhaale ki nok, chamde ki chappalein aur angoothiyan bhi milin.

## Lok katha kya kehti hai?

Sthaniya manyata ke anusaar Kannauj ke **Raja Jasdhaval** apni garbhvati Rani **Balampa**, sevakon aur nartakiyon ke saath **Nanda Devi** ki yatra par nikle the. Kaha jaata hai ki yatra mein niyamon ka paalan nahi hua aur Devi naraaz ho gayin. Roopkund ke paas bhayankar **olavrishti** hui aur poora dal wahin sama gaya.

Pahad ke lokgeeton mein aaj bhi is katha ka zikr milta hai. Roopkund, **Nanda Devi Raj Jat** ke raaste mein hi padta hai.

## Pehle vaigyanikon ne kya socha?

Shuruaati jaanch mein kai khopdiyon par upar se giri kisi gol cheez ki chot jaise nishaan dikhe. Isse kuch shodhkartaon ne maana ki log sachmuch **olon ke toofan** mein mare honge — theek waise hi jaise lok katha kehti hai. Sabko ek hi ghatna ka shikaar maana gaya.

## DNA jaanch ne kya bataya?

2019 mein vaigyanikon ne **38 kankalon** ki DNA aur radiocarbon jaanch ki. Nateeje chaunkane wale the:

| Samooh | Log | Kab mare (lagbhag) | Vansh |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pehla | 23 | San 800 ke aaspaas | Dakshin Asiai |
| Doosra | 14 | San 1800 ke aaspaas | Poorvi Bhoomadhyasagar (aaj ke Greece/Crete jaise) |
| Teesra | 1 | San 1800 ke aaspaas | Dakshin-Poorv Asiai |

Yaani ye log **ek saath nahi mare**. Yahan kam se kam **do alag-alag ghatnayein** huin, jinke beech lagbhag **1000 saal** ka faasla hai. Purane, Dakshin Asiai logon ke avshesh shayad lambe samay mein alag-alag maukon par jama hue.

## To rahasya ab bhi kyon hai?

Vigyan ne bata diya ki log **kaun** the aur **kab** mare, lekin kai sawaal ab bhi khule hain:

- Bhoomadhyasagar ke log 200 saal pehle itni durgam jagah par kya kar rahe the?
- Dono samoohon ki maut kis wajah se hui — ole, barfeela toofan ya kuch aur?

Yahin **lok katha aur vigyan** dono apni-apni jagah khade hain. Katha humein pahad ki yatra ke niyamon aur Devi ke samman ki seekh deti hai, aur vigyan batata hai ki Himalaya ne sadiyon mein kitne alag-alag yatriyon ko apni god mein dekha hai.

## Aaj ki chinta

Reports ke anusaar kuch paryatak aur trekker yahan se haddiyan uthakar le jaate rahe hain, jisse ye avshesh dheere-dheere kam ho rahe hain. Prashasan is kshetra ko sanrakshit karne ki baat karta raha hai.

> Roopkund jaayein to sirf yaadein lekar lautein — yahan ki ek bhi haddi ya patthar nahi.
