Samudra tal se lagbhag 3,583 metre ki oonchai par, barfeeli chotiyon ke beech khada **Kedarnath Mandir** barah jyotirlingon mein se ek hai. Lekin yahan Shivling baaki jyotirlingon jaisa nahi — yeh ek **trikoniya pind** hai, jaise kisi bail ki peeth ka koobad. Iske peechhe ek adbhut katha hai.

## Pandav Shiv ko kyon khoj rahe the?

Mahabharat yuddh mein Pandavon ke haathon apne hi bhaiyon aur guruon ki mrityu hui thi. Is paap se mukti ke liye woh **Bhagwan Shiv** ka aashirwad chahte the. Lekin Shiv unse naraaz the aur unhe darshan nahi dena chahte the.

Shiv Kashi se nikalkar Himalaya aa gaye aur **Guptkashi** mein chhip gaye. (Isiliye us jagah ka naam "Gupt" Kashi pada.)

## Bail roop mein Shiv

Pandav unka peechha karte hue wahan bhi pahunch gaye. Tab Shiv ne **bail (Nandi) ka roop** dhaaran kiya aur pashuon ke jhund mein mil gaye.

**Bheem** ne ek chaal chali. Unhone apne dono pair do pahadon par phaila diye. Saare pashu unke pairon ke neeche se nikal gaye, par ek bail nahi nikla. Pandav samajh gaye ki yahi Shiv hain.

Bail zameen mein samaane laga. Bheem ne jhapatkar uski **peeth (koobad)** pakad li. Shiv Pandavon ki lagan se prasann hue, unhe darshan diye aur paap se mukt kiya.

## Panch Kedar ka janm

Manyata hai ki bail ke shareer ke alag-alag ang paanch jagahon par prakat hue. Yahi hain **Panch Kedar**:

| Sthaan | Ang |
|---|---|
| **Kedarnath** | Peeth (koobad) |
| **Tungnath** | Bhujayein |
| **Rudranath** | Mukh |
| **Madhyamaheshwar** | Naabhi |
| **Kalpeshwar** | Jataayein |

Kaha jaata hai ki bail ka **sir Nepal** mein prakat hua, jahan **Pashupatinath** mandir hai.

## Mandir kisne banwaya?

Manyata hai ki mandir ki neev Pandavon ne rakhi thi, aur baad mein **Adi Shankaracharya** ne iska jeernoddhar karaya. Mandir ke peechhe aaj bhi Shankaracharya ki samadhi hai.

## 2013 mein chamatkar?

2013 ki aapda mein jab sab kuch beh gaya, tab mandir ke peechhe ek **badi chattan** aakar ruk gayi aur paani-malbe ka bahaav do hisson mein bant gaya. Mandir bach gaya. Aaj log us chattan ko **"Bheem Shila"** kehkar poojte hain.
