Almora se lagbhag 8 kilometre door, cheed ke pedon ke beech ek mandir hai jahan pahunchte hi ghantiyon ki awaaz aapka swagat karti hai. Chhoti, badi, peetal ki, taambe ki — hazaron ghantiyan. Aur unke beech bandhe hain kaagaz ke tukde, jin par logon ne apni pareshani likhi hai. Yeh hai **Chitai Golu Devta Mandir**.

## Kaun hain Golu Devta?

Kumaon mein Golu Devta ko **nyay ka devta** maana jaata hai. Unhe Gwel, Goril, Goljyu ya Gwalla Devta bhi kehte hain. Manyata hai ki woh Bhagwan Shiv ke Gaur Bhairav roop ke avatar hain aur aaj bhi apne bhakton ke saath nyay karte hain.

## Janm ki katha

Lok katha ke anusaar Champawat ke Raja **Jhal Rai** ki saat raniyan thi, par koi santaan nahi thi. Baad mein unhone **Kalinka** se vivah kiya aur unse ek putra hua. Saaton raniyon ko jalan hui. Unhone navjaat bachche ko hatakar uski jagah ek patthar rakh diya aur bachche ko ek bakse mein band karke nadi mein baha diya.

Woh baksa ek machhuare ko mila, jisne bachche ko apne bete ki tarah paala.

## Lakdi ka ghoda jo paani peeta tha

Baalak bada hua to use sach ka pata chala. Ek din woh **lakdi ka ghoda** lekar usi ghaat par pahuncha jahan saaton raniyan naha rahi thi, aur ghode ko paani pilane laga. Raniyan hansi — "Kya lakdi ka ghoda bhi paani peeta hai?"

Baalak ne jawab diya:

> "Jab koi rani patthar ko janm de sakti hai, to lakdi ka ghoda paani kyon nahi pee sakta?"

Yeh baat Raja tak pahunchi, sach saamne aaya aur raniyon ko dand mila. Yahi baalak aage chalkar nyaypriya raja bana aur log unhe **Golu Devta** ke roop mein poojne lage.

## Arzi aur ghantiyan kyon?

Golu Devta ke mandir ki sabse anokhi parampara hai **arzi likhna**. Bhakt saade kaagaz par — kai log to stamp paper par bhi — apni shikayat ya manokamna likhkar mandir mein taang dete hain, theek waise jaise adaalat mein arzi di jaati hai.

Manyata hai ki jab manokamna poori ho jaati hai, to bhakt lautkar **ghanti chadhate** hain. Isiliye Chitai mandir mein ghantiyon ka ambaar laga hai.

## Kahan-kahan hain mandir?

- **Chitai** (Almora) — sabse prasiddh
- **Ghorakhal** (Nainital zila) — yahan bhi ghantiyon ke liye mashhoor
- **Champawat** — jahan se katha shuru hoti hai
