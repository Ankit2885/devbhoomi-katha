🙏 Uttarakhand Devbhoomi hai aur yahan ke kan-kan mein devi-devtaon ka vaas hai. Vigat 10 aur 11 November ko kshetra ki aaradhya Devi ka **Kanda Mela** badli hui paramparaon ke saath sampann hua.

## Devi aaj vivash kyon dikhti hain?

Jo Devi hamari raksha karne wali hain, buddhi dene wali hain, kaal ke phaans ko kaatne wali hain aur shaadi-vivah ke rishte aadi shubh karya sampann karane wali hain — aaj woh maano vivash hokar sab kuch dekh rahi hain. Ab Mata ke mandir mein **chhatra wala dhwaj** chadhaya jaata hai, jis par Hanuman ji viraajmaan hote hain. Yeh vyavastha mool roop se keval un logon ke liye thi jo poornatah shakahari hain.

## Chowkidaron ka hissa

Aaj kshetra ki janta jitni dukhi hai, utni hi peeda mein swayam hamari kshetra ki Devi bhi hain, kyonki woh pehle ki tarah raksha nahi kar pa rahi hain. Lok manyata ke anusaar, ab unke paas apne **chowkidaron** (rakshak ganon/vanyajeevon) ko dene ke liye unka hissa (bhog) nahi pahunch raha hai. Parinaamswaroop, ye 'chowkidar' (guldar/sher) apni manmarzi se insaan aur jaanwar sabhi ko ek samaan maankar unka shikaar kar rahe hain. Ab sabhi keh rahe hain ki sher ko maar daalo, lekin woh bhi to ek bezubaan jeev hai.

## Bali ki parampara aur badli hui pooja-paddhati

Jab bezubaanon ko antatah insaan ke dwara hi maara jaana hai, to Uttarakhand ki prachin aastha aur Devbhoomi mein prakriti ka santulan banaye rakhne ke liye Mata ke mandir mein bali ki parampara thi. Tab logon ne bezubaan jaanwaron par daya dikhate hue Mata ki pooja-paddhati aur parampara ko badal diya.

Vidambana yeh hai ki aaj log apne swaad aur khaane ke liye to bakre paal rahe hain, lekin Mata ke bhog ke naam par daya dikhate hain. Doosri or, Mata maano un logon ko dand dene ke liye hi in vanyajeevon (sher/guldar) ko paal rahi hain, jo apne swaarth ke liye bezubaan jaanwaron ko kaatkar kha rahe hain.

> "Chit bhi meri aur pat bhi meri" nahi ho sakti.

Yadi parampara badalni hi thi, to pehle poore Uttarakhand ko poornatah shakahari rajya ghoshit karna chahiye tha, uske baad hi Mata ki pooja-paddhati mein parivartan kiya jaana uchit tha.

## Aajeevika ka sawaal

Halanki, yahan ek vyavaharik prashn yeh bhi khada hota hai ki Uttarakhand mein **bakri paalan** logon ke rozgaar ka ek pramukh saadhan hai. Yadi iska upyog nahi hoga, to yeh aajeevika ka madhyam kaise banega?

## Nar ya Narayani?

Prakriti jab apna raudra roop dikhati hai, to woh kisi bhi madhyam se apna nyay kar sakti hai. Aise mein humein Mata ke anek roop dekhne ko milenge. Uttarakhand dharti ka swarg hai, aur yahan ki paristhitiyon ka achha ya bura prabhav poore desh par padta hai.

Ab dekhna yeh hai ki antatah pehle kaun jhukta hai — **Nar ya Narayani?**

**Jai Shri Ram 🙏🙏**
