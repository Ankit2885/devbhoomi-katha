Kullu ghati ke upar ek pahad ki choti par lakdi ka chhota-sa mandir hai, jiske saamne **devdar ka ek bahut ooncha stambh** khada hai. Ek taraf **Kullu ghati**, doosri taraf **Parvati ghati**. Yeh hai **Bijli Mahadev** — jahan ki sabse badi manyata hai ki **aasmaan se bijli seedhe Shivling par girti hai**.

## Kahan hai Bijli Mahadev?

Mandir Himachal Pradesh ke **Kullu** zile mein **Kashawri gaon** ke paas, lagbhag **2,460 metre** ki oonchai par hai. Sadak khatam hone ke baad kareeb **3 kilometre** ka paidal raasta hai. Trekking pasand karne wale **Naggar** ki taraf se **Jana gaon** hote hue devdar ke ghane junglon se bhi yahan pahunchte hain.

## Katha: Jab Shiv ne ghati ko doobne se bachaya

Lok katha ke anusaar ek asur ne poori ghati ko paani mein dubo dene ki thaan li thi. Ek roop mein uska naam **Kulant** bataya jaata hai, jo vishaal **ajgar** bankar Lahaul-Spiti ki or se aaya aur nadi ka raasta rokkar sab kuch dubona chahta tha. Ek doosri katha mein yeh asur **Jalandhar** hai.

Dono kathaon mein **Bhagwan Shiv** ne asur ka ant kiya aur ghati ke logon ko bachaya. Kai log maante hain ki **"Kullu"** naam bhi isi **Kulant** ki katha se juda hai.

## Bijli kyon girti hai? — Manyata

Manyata hai ki Shiv bhakton par aane wale sankat ko **bijli ke roop mein apne upar le lete hain**. Kaha jaata hai ki lagbhag **har 12 saal** mein bijli girkar Shivling ko tukdon mein tod deti hai.

Iske baad mandir ke pujari **ghar ke bane makkhan**, daal ke aate aur anaaj ke mishran se toote tukdon ko phir se jodte hain, aur Shivling pehle jaisa ho jaata hai.

## Vigyan kya kehta hai?

- Mandir ek **khuli, oonchi choti** par hai aur uske saamne **bahut ooncha lakdi ka stambh** hai. Aisi jagahon par aakashiya bijli girne ki sambhavna waise bhi zyada hoti hai — bijli aksar aaspaas ki sabse oonchi cheez par girti hai.
- Pahadon mein toofan aur garaj wale baadal aam hain, khaaskar barsaat mein.

Yaani vigyan batata hai ki bijli **yahin kyon** girti hai, aur aastha batati hai ki bhakt ise **Shiv ki kripa** ke roop mein kyon dekhte hain. Dono baatein saath-saath chalti hain.

## Mandir ki banavat

- Mandir **Kath-Kuni shaili** mein bana hai — lakdi aur patthar ki parton wali paramparik pahadi banavat.
- Garbhgriha ke chaaron or lakdi ka **aayatakar galiyara** hai, jahan shraddhalu parikrama karte hain.
- Kinaron par **Shiv** aur **Nandi** ki patthar ki murtiyan hain.
- Maana jaata hai ki yeh mandir **Pandavon** ne vanvaas ke samay banaya tha.

## Kab jaayein?

- **April se June** aur **September se November** achha samay maana jaata hai.
- **Saawan** mein bhakton ki bheed rehti hai.
- Sardiyon mein poora pahad barf se dhak jaata hai.
