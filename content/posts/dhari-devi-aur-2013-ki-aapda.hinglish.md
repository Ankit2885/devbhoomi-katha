Srinagar (Garhwal) se kuch kilometre aage, **Alaknanda nadi** ke beech ek chattan par viraajmaan hain **Maa Dhari Devi**. Garhwal ke log unhe **Chardham ki rakshak** maante hain. 2013 ke baad yeh mandir poore desh mein charcha ka vishay ban gaya. Kyon? Aaiye samajhte hain.

## Manyata kya hai?

Lok manyata ke anusaar Dhari Devi ki murti ka **upari bhaag** yahan pooja jaata hai, jabki **nichla bhaag** Kalimath mein maana jaata hai. Kaha jaata hai ki Devi din mein teen roop badalti hain — subah kanya, dopahar yuvati aur shaam ko vriddha.

Sabse gehri manyata yeh hai ki **Devi ko unke mool sthaan se hatana ashubh hai**.

## 16 June 2013 ko kya hua?

Alaknanda par ban rahi **Srinagar hydro power project** ki jheel mein purana mandir doobne wala tha. Isliye 16 June 2013 ki shaam murti ko uske mool sthaan se hatakar upar ek asthayi jagah par rakha gaya.

Usi raat aur agle din Kedarghati mein woh bheeshan aapda aayi jismein hazaron logon ki jaan gayi. Bahut se logon ke liye yeh sanyog nahi, balki **Devi ka krodh** tha.

## Vigyan kya kehta hai?

Vaigyanikon aur sarkari reports ne aapda ke ye kaaran bataye:

- **Samay se pehle aur bahut bhaari monsoon baarish** — June ke beech mein kai dinon tak lagataar baarish hui
- **Chorabari Taal** (Kedarnath ke upar ki jheel) ka tootna, jisse achanak bhaari malba aur paani neeche aaya
- Nadiyon ke kinare **aniyojit nirmaan**, jisse nuksaan kai guna badh gaya

Yaani vigyan ki nazar mein yeh prakritik aapda thi, jise insaani galtiyon ne aur bhayanak bana diya.

## To sach kya hai?

Yahin aastha aur vigyan alag-alag raaste par chalte hain. Vigyan baarish aur glacier jheel ki baat karta hai. Aastha kehti hai ki **pahadon ka santulan bigaadne ka parinaam** yahi hota hai.

Dilchasp baat yeh hai ki dono ek baat par sahmat hain — **pahadon ke saath chhedchhaad mehngi padti hai**. Shayad hamare poorvajon ne yahi baat aastha ke roop mein humein samjhayi thi.

## Aaj ka mandir

Ab nadi ke beech oonche khambhon par naya mandir bana hai aur 2023 mein Devi ko vidhi-vidhaan se naye mandir mein sthaapit kiya gaya. Har saal laakhon shraddhalu darshan ke liye aate hain.
