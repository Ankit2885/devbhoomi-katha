**Rudraprayag, Garhwal.** Vishwa prasiddh **Kedarnath Dham** ke kapaat **Budhvaar, 11 November 2026** ko **Bhaiya Dooj** ke paavan din, subah lagbhag **saadhe chhah baje** vaidik mantrochchaar aur vidhi-vidhaan ke saath **sheetkaal ke liye band** kar diye gaye.

## Doli pahunchi Omkareshwar Mandir

Kapaat band hone ke baad **Baba Kedar ki Panchmukhi utsav doli** hazaron bhakton ke jaykaron ke beech apne sheetkaleen padaav ke liye ravana hui. Raaste ke padaavon se hote hue doli **13 November** ko **Ukhimath** ke **Omkareshwar Mandir** pahunchi aur wahan viraajmaan hui.

Ab agle **lagbhag chhah mahine** Baba Kedar ke darshan **Omkareshwar Mandir, Ukhimath** mein hi honge.

## Kapaat sheetkaal mein kyon band hote hain?

Kedarnath lagbhag **3,583 metre** ki oonchai par hai. Sardiyon mein yahan kai foot barf jam jaati hai aur raaste band ho jaate hain. Isliye har saal **Bhaiya Dooj** ke aaspaas kapaat band hote hain aur Bhagwan ki pooja neeche **Ukhimath** mein hoti hai. Agle saal kapaat khulne ki tareekh **Mahashivratri** par ghoshit ki jaati hai.

## Sheetkaleen yatra

Jo bhakt sardiyon mein darshan karna chahte hain, ve **Omkareshwar Mandir, Ukhimath** ja sakte hain. Yeh sadak maarg se juda hai aur Guptkashi ke paas hai.
