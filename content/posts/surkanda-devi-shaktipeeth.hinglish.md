Mussoorie se Dhanaulti ki or badhte hue, pahad ki ek oonchi choti par laal dhwaj lehrata dikhta hai. Yeh hai **Maa Surkanda Devi** ka mandir — **Tehri Garhwal** ka prasiddh **siddhpeeth**.

## Kahan hai mandir?

Surkanda Devi mandir **Tehri Garhwal zile** mein **Dhanaulti** ke paas, lagbhag **2,756 metre** ki oonchai par hai. Saaf mausam mein yahan se Himalaya ki barfeeli chotiyon ki lambi kataar dikhai deti hai.

## Sati ki katha

Puranon ke anusaar Raja **Daksh** ki putri **Sati** Bhagwan Shiv ki patni thin. Daksh ne ek bada yagya kiya, par Shiv ka apmaan kiya. Apmaan sahan na kar paane ke kaaran Sati ne yagya ki agni mein swayam ko samarpit kar diya.

Dukhi Shiv Sati ka shareer lekar poore brahmand mein ghoomne lage. Srishti ka santulan bigadta dekh Bhagwan **Vishnu** ne apne **Sudarshan Chakra** se Sati ke shareer ke ang alag kar diye. Jahan-jahan ye ang gire, ve sthaan **Shaktipeeth** kehlaye.

## Surkanda ka naam aur manyata

Manyata hai ki **Surkanda mein Sati ka sir (sir ka bhaag)** gira tha. Isiliye is sthaan ko pehle **"Sirkanda"** kaha jaata tha, jo samay ke saath **Surkanda** ho gaya — aisa sthaniya log batate hain.

Bhakt maante hain ki sachche mann se yahan maangi gayi manokamna poori hoti hai.

## Tehri ki teen Deviyan

Garhwal mein ek prachalit manyata hai ki Tehri kshetra ki teen pramukh Deviyan — **Surkanda Devi**, **Kunjapuri Devi** aur **Chandrabadni Devi** — ek divya trikon banati hain. Kai shraddhalu teenon ke darshan ek saath karna shubh maante hain.

## Yatra se judi baatein

- Mandir tak pahunchne ke liye sadak se kuch doori paidal chadhni padti hai.
- Oonchai ke kaaran yahan mausam jaldi badalta hai — garam kapde saath rakhein.
- Sardiyon mein yahan aksar barf girti hai.

> **Jai Maa Surkanda!** — pahad ki choti se goonjta yeh jaykara poore ilaake ki aastha ka prateek hai.
