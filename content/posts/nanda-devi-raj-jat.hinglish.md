Uttarakhand mein **Maa Nanda** sirf devi nahi, ghar ki **beti** hain. Garhwal aur Kumaon — dono unhe apni dhyan (vivahit beti) maante hain. Aur jab beti maayke se sasural jaati hai, to poora gaon use vida karne jaata hai. Yahi hai **Nanda Devi Raj Jat**.

## Katha kya hai?

Manyata hai ki Maa Nanda ka vivah Bhagwan Shiv se hua aur unka sasural **Kailash** hai. Ek baar woh apne maayke aayi. Kuch samay baad unhe sasural lautna tha. Tab poore kshetra ke logon ne gaaje-baaje ke saath unhe Himalaya tak chhodne ka sankalp liya.

Tab se yeh parampara chali aa rahi hai — **beti Nanda ko Kailash tak vida karna**.

## Chausingya Khadu — yatra ka aguwa

Is yatra ki sabse anokhi baat hai **chaar seeng wala medha**, jise sthaniya bhasha mein **Chausingya Khadu** kehte hain. Manyata hai ki Raj Jat ke samay kshetra mein aisa medha janm leta hai aur wahi yatra ki aguwai karta hai.

Yatra ke ant mein **Homkund** ke paas uski peeth par Devi ka shringaar aur bhent baandhkar use akele Himalaya ki or chhod diya jaata hai. Log maante hain ki woh Maa Nanda ka saamaan lekar Kailash jaata hai.

## Yatra ka raasta

- Shuruaat: **Nauti gaon** (Karnaprayag ke paas, Chamoli)
- Lambai: lagbhag **280 kilometre**, kareeb **19-20 din**
- Raaste mein ghane jungle, bugyal aur oonche darre
- **Roopkund** — rahasyamayi kankalon wali jheel — bhi isi raaste par padti hai
- Antim padaav: **Homkund**

## Itne saal mein ek baar kyon?

Raj Jat parampara ke anusaar lagbhag **har 12 saal** mein hoti hai, halanki alag-alag kaaranon se ismein deri bhi hoti rahi hai. Pichhli badi Raj Jat **2014** mein hui thi. Har saal hone wali chhoti **Nanda Jat** alag hai.

## Kyon hai yeh itni khaas?

Raj Jat ko **Himalaya ki sabse lambi dharmik paidal yatraon** mein gina jaata hai. Lekin iska asli matlab bhavnatmak hai. Jab yatri Homkund par Khadu ko vida karte hain, to kai logon ki aankhein bhar aati hain — theek waise hi jaise beti ki vidaai par.

> "Nanda Devi ki Jai!" — yeh jaykara poore raaste pahadon mein goonjta hai.
