Alaknanda kinare base **Srinagar (Pauri Garhwal)** me ek raat aisi aati hai jab poora shehar jaagta hai. Mandir ke aangan me saikdon diye timtimate hain aur kai dampati poori raat **haath me jalta diya lekar khade** rehte hain. Ye raat hai **Baikunth Chaturdashi** ki, aur jagah hai Garhwal ka prachin **Kamleshwar Mahadev mandir**.

## Baikunth Chaturdashi 2026 kab hai?

- **Tareekh:** Somvaar, **23 November 2026** (Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi)
- **Khada Diya anushthan:** 23 November ki raat, Kamleshwar Mahadev mandir me
- **Baikunth Chaturdashi mela, Srinagar:** 23 November se lagbhag 10 din (2 December tak)

> Panchang ke anusaar Chaturdashi tithi 23 November ko subah lagbhag 2:36 baje se raat lagbhag 11:42 baje tak rahegi. Pooja ka sahi samay apne purohit ya mandir samiti se zaroor poochh lein.

## Khada Diya kya hai?

**Khada Diya** Kamleshwar Mahadev mandir ka sabse prasiddh anushthan hai. Manyata hai ki jin dampatiyon ko **santan ki kaamna** hai, ve Baikunth Chaturdashi ki raat Bhagwan Shiv ke saamne **haath me jalta diya lekar poori raat khade** rehkar tap karte hain. Subah snaan-pooja ke baad anushthan poora hota hai.

Bhakt maante hain ki sachche mann se kiya gaya ye tap Bholenath tak zaroor pahunchta hai. Isiliye har saal Garhwal hi nahi, desh ke doosre rajyon se bhi dampati yahan pahunchte hain.

### Is baar videsh se bhi aa rahe hain dampati

Amar Ujala ki 4 September 2026 ki report ke anusaar is baar anushthan ke liye **England aur America se ek-ek dampati** ne bhi registration karaya tha, aur tab tak kul **30 dampati** register ho chuke the. Mandir ke Mahant **108 Ashutosh Puri** hain. Registration mandir me jaakar aur phone se bhi hota hai — isliye jaane se pehle mandir samiti se sampark kar lein.

## Naam "Kamleshwar" kyon? — Sahasra Kamal ki katha

![Sudarshan Chakra, netra aur ek hazaar kamal — Kamleshwar ki katha ka chitra (chitra)](/images/posts/kamleshwar-sahastra-kamal.jpg)
*Manyata: ek kamal kam pada to Bhagwan Vishnu ne apna netra arpit kiya (chitra)*

Lok manyata ke anusaar ek baar Bhagwan **Vishnu** ne yahan Bhagwan **Shiv** ki pooja **ek hazaar kamal** chadhakar karne ka sankalp liya. Shiv ne pareeksha lene ke liye ek kamal chhupa diya. Ginti me ek kamal kam pada, to Vishnu ne — jinhe **"Kamalnayan"** kaha jaata hai — kamal ki jagah **apna ek netra** arpit karne ka nishchay kiya.

Bhakti dekhkar Shiv prasann hue aur Vishnu ko **Sudarshan Chakra** pradaan kiya. Manyata hai ki ye ghatna **Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi** ko hui — isliye ye din **Baikunth Chaturdashi** kehlata hai aur is din Shiv aur Vishnu dono ki pooja hoti hai. Kamal se pooja ke kaaran hi ye sthaan **Kamleshwar** kehlaya.

Ek anya lok manyata me Bhagwan **Ram** ka bhi yahan Shiv pooja karna bataya jaata hai. Aisi kathayein peedhiyon se suni-sunaayi jaati hain aur mandir ki aastha ka hissa hain.

## Srinagar ka Baikunth Chaturdashi Mela 2026

Baikunth Chaturdashi ke saath Srinagar me bada **mela** bhi lagta hai, jise Nagar Nigam Srinagar aayojit karta hai. Is baar mela **23 November se lagbhag 10 din** chalega. Udghatan ke din bhajan sandhya hoti hai, aur agle dinon me jhaankiyan, sanskritik karyakram aur pratiyogitayein hoti hain. Khabron ke anusaar is baar mele ki jaankari aur parking ke liye **QR code** ki vyavastha bhi ki gayi hai.

## Jaane se pehle dhyaan rakhein

- November ki raat pahad me thandi hoti hai — garam kapde, shawl aur paani saath rakhein.
- Khada Diya ke liye **pehle se registration** karayein aur niyam mandir se samajh lein.
- Baikunth Chaturdashi ki raat bheed bahut hoti hai — bachchon aur buzurgon ka dhyaan rakhein.
- Srinagar Rishikesh–Badrinath maarg par hai; Rishikesh se sadak ke raaste aasaani se pahuncha ja sakta hai.

Garhwal ke doosre Shiv dhaamon ke baare me bhi padhein — [Kedarnath aur Panch Kedar](/katha/kedarnath-aur-panch-kedar) aur [Tungnath: duniya ka sabse ooncha Shiv mandir](/katha/tungnath-sabse-uncha-shiv-mandir). Garhwal ki saari kathayein [yahan](/kshetra/garhwal) hain.

## Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

### Baikunth Chaturdashi 2026 kab hai?
Baikunth Chaturdashi 2026 Somvaar, 23 November ko hai. Ye Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi ko manayi jaati hai.

### Kamleshwar Mahadev mandir kahan hai?
Kamleshwar Mahadev mandir Uttarakhand ke Pauri Garhwal zile ke Srinagar shehar me Alaknanda nadi ke paas sthit hai.

### Khada Diya anushthan kya hota hai?
Santan ki kaamna wale dampati Baikunth Chaturdashi ki raat Kamleshwar Mahadev mandir me poori raat haath me jalta diya lekar khade rehte hain aur Bhagwan Shiv se prarthna karte hain. Subah pooja ke baad anushthan poora hota hai.

### Khada Diya ke liye registration kaise hota hai?
Registration mandir samiti ke paas hota hai — mandir me jaakar ya phone se. Sahi prakriya aur niyam ke liye pehle mandir se sampark karein.

### Srinagar Baikunth Chaturdashi mela 2026 kab tak chalega?
Mela 23 November 2026 se lagbhag 10 din, yaani kareeb 2 December tak chalega.
