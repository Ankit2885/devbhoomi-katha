Punjab ke Anandpur Sahib ki or se Himachal me daakhil hote hi door ek oonchi pahadi par safed-sunehra mandir dikhta hai, aur neeche faili hoti hai neeli **Gobind Sagar** jheel. Yahi hai **Shri Naina Devi** — Himachal Pradesh ke **Bilaspur zile** ka sabse bada teerth aur desh ke prasiddh **Shaktipeethon** me se ek.

## Kahan hai Naina Devi mandir?

- **Zila:** Bilaspur, Himachal Pradesh (kasbe ka naam bhi "Naina Devi" hai)
- **Sthaan:** Pahadi ki choti par, neeche Bhakra baandh se bani **Gobind Sagar jheel**
- **Pahunch:** Sadak se pahadi ke ek hisse tak, phir seedhiyan — aur neeche se upar tak **ropeway (cable car)** ki suvidha bhi hai
- **Nazdeeki:** Punjab ka Anandpur Sahib aur Himachal ka Swarghat

## Sati ke netra ki katha

![Maa Sati ke netra ka prateek, diye aur kamal (chitra)](/images/posts/naina-devi-sati-netra.jpg)
*Manyata: yahan Maa Sati ka netra gira tha, isliye naam pada "Naina" Devi (chitra)*

Devi Bhagwat Puran ki katha ke anusaar Raja **Daksh** ke yagya me Bhagwan Shiv ka apmaan sahan na kar paane par **Sati** ne yagya ki agni me deh tyaag di. Dukhi Shiv Sati ka shareer lekar taandav karne lage. Srishti ko bachane ke liye Bhagwan **Vishnu** ne Sati ke shareer ke ang alag kiye, aur jahan-jahan ve gire, wahan **Shaktipeeth** bane.

Manyata hai ki **Naina Devi me Sati ka netra** (prachalit manyata me daayan netra) gira tha — isiliye Devi ka naam **"Naina"** Devi pada.

## Gwale Naina aur safed gaay ki kahani

Ek lok katha kehti hai ki kisi aapda me mandir lupt ho gaya tha. Bahut samay baad **Naina** naam ka ek **Gujjar gwala** yahan pashu chara raha tha. Usne dekha ki ek **safed gaay** roz ek patthar par apne thanon se doodh gira deti hai. Kai din yahi hota raha.

Ek raat Devi ne use sapne me darshan dekar bataya ki wo patthar unhi ki **pindi** hai. Naina ne ye baat **Raja Bir Chand** ko batayi. Raja ne khud ye chamatkaar dekha aur usi sthaan par mandir banwaya — aur gwale ke naam par hi mandir ka naam pada.

## "Mahishpeeth" kyon kehlata hai?

Ek aur katha ke anusaar yahi wo sthaan hai jahan Devi ne **Mahishasur** ka vadh kiya. Brahma ke vardaan se Mahishasur ko keval ek kunwari kanya hi hara sakti thi. Devtaon ki shaktiyon se prakat hui Devi se Mahishasur ne vivaah ka prastaav rakha. Devi ne kaha — "Yuddh me mujhe hara do, to vivaah karungi." Yuddh me Devi ne Mahishasur ko haraya aur uski **dono aankhein** nikaal deen. Devtaon ne prasann hokar **"Jai Naina!"** ka jaykara lagaya. Isliye is sthaan ko **Mahishpeeth** bhi kaha jaata hai.

## Navratri aur Saawan ke mele

**Chaitra aur Ashwin Navratri** tatha **Saawan** me yahan vishaal mela lagta hai aur Punjab, Haryana, Himachal samet kai rajyon se laakhon shraddhalu aate hain.

Bheed ke samay saavdhani bahut zaroori hai — **3 August 2008** ko Saawan ke dauraan yahan bhagdad me sau se zyada shraddhaluon ki jaan gayi thi. Tab se bheed prabandhan par khaas dhyaan diya jaata hai.

## Yatra ke liye zaroori baatein

- Navratri aur chhuttiyon me bahut bheed hoti hai — subah jaldi pahunchein.
- Seedhiyan chadhni hon to aaramdayak joote pehnein; buzurgon ke liye ropeway behtar hai.
- Nazdeek ka **Shri Anandpur Sahib** (Punjab) bhi kai shraddhalu ek saath ghoomte hain.
- Himachal ke doosre prasiddh Devi dhaam — [Kangra ki Bajreshwari Devi (Nagarkot Dham)](/katha/bajreshwari-devi-kangra-nagarkot-dham), Jwala Ji, Chintpurni aur Chamunda Devi — bhi kai shraddhalu ek hi yatra me karte hain.

## Nainital ki Naina Devi se alag

Bahut log khojte hue bhramit ho jaate hain — **Uttarakhand ke Nainital** me Naini jheel ke kinare bhi **Naina Devi mandir** hai. Ye lekh **Himachal ke Bilaspur** wali Shri Naina Devi ke baare me hai. Dono hi aastha ke bade kendra hain.

Himachal ki aur kathayein padhein — [Hidimba Devi mandir, Manali](/katha/hidimba-devi-mandir-manali) aur [Bijli Mahadev ka rahasya](/katha/bijli-mahadev-kullu-rahasya). Saari kathayein [Himachal page](/kshetra/himachal) par hain.

## Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

### Naina Devi mandir Himachal me kahan hai?
Shri Naina Devi mandir Himachal Pradesh ke Bilaspur zile me ek pahadi ki choti par hai, jahan se Gobind Sagar jheel dikhti hai. Ye Punjab ke Anandpur Sahib ke nazdeek hai.

### Naina Devi me Sati ka kaun-sa ang gira tha?
Manyata hai ki yahan Maa Sati ka netra gira tha, isliye Devi ka naam Naina Devi pada. Prachalit manyata me ise daayan netra bataya jaata hai.

### Naina Devi mandir tak ropeway hai kya?
Haan, pahadi ke neeche se mandir tak cable car (ropeway) ki suvidha hai. Seedhiyon se bhi paidal chadha ja sakta hai.

### Naina Devi jaane ka sabse achha samay kab hai?
Mandir saal bhar khula rehta hai. Navratri aur Saawan me mela lagta hai, isliye shaanti se darshan karne hon to saamanya dinon me subah jaldi jaayein.

### Kya Nainital aur Bilaspur ki Naina Devi ek hi hain?
Nahi, ye do alag mandir hain. Ek Uttarakhand ke Nainital me Naini jheel ke kinare hai aur doosra Himachal ke Bilaspur zile me pahadi par.
