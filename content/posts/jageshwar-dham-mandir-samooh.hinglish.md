Oonche-oonche **devdar** ke ped, unke beech behti chhoti-si **Jataganga** nadi, aur patthar ke bane darjanon chhote-bade shikhar. Almora zile ka **Jageshwar Dham** pehli nazar mein hi kisi aur yug mein le jaata hai.

## Kahan hai Jageshwar?

Jageshwar, **Almora se lagbhag 36 kilometre uttar-poorv** mein Kumaon kshetra mein hai. Mandir samooh kareeb **1,870 metre** ki oonchai par, devdar ke jungle se ghiri **Jataganga ghati** mein basa hai. Almora–Pithoragarh marg par **Artola** gaon se ek sadak alag hokar yahan pahunchti hai.

## 125 mandir — ek hi jagah par

Jageshwar mein ek-do nahi, balki **lagbhag 125 prachin mandiron** ka samooh hai. Itihaaskaar inhe **7vin se 14vin sadi** ke beech ka maante hain. Ye sabhi **kate hue pattharon** se bane hain — kuch bahut chhote, kuch kaafi bade.

Zyadatar mandir **Bhagwan Shiv** ko samarpit hain, lekin aaspaas Vishnu, Shakti (Devi) aur Surya ke mandir bhi hain. Yahan ke pramukh mandiron mein shaamil hain:

- **Jageshwar Mandir**
- **Dandeshwar Mandir**
- **Mrityunjay Mandir**
- **Kuber Mandir**
- **Chandi ka Mandir**
- **Nanda Devi / Nav Durga** Mandir
- **Navgrah** aur **Surya** Mandir

## Nagar shaili kya hai?

Jageshwar ke zyadatar mandir Uttar Bharat ki **Nagar shaili** mein bane hain. Iski pehchaan hai upar ki or dheere-dheere patla hota **ooncha shikhar**, uske upar gol, dhaaridaar patthar (**aamlak**) aur sabse upar **kalash**. Kuch mandiron mein Dakshin aur Madhya Bharat ki shaili ki jhalak bhi milti hai.

## Jyotirlinga ki manyata

**Dwadash Jyotirlinga Stotra** ke ek paath ke aadhaar par kai shraddhalu Jageshwar ko barah jyotirlingon se jodte hain. Is par vidvanon mein alag-alag mat hain, lekin Kumaon ke logon ke liye Jageshwar hamesha se **Shiv ka dhaam** raha hai.

## Inhe kisne banwaya?

Yeh sawaal aaj bhi poori tarah hal nahi hua hai. Angrezon ke samay kuch logon ne inhe **Katyuri** ya **Chand** rajvansh se joda, lekin iske pakke shilalekhi pramaan nahi mile. Sthapatya ke aadhaar par maana jaata hai ki kuch mandir pehli sahasrabdi ke uttarardh ke hain aur kuch baad ke. Shodh batata hai ki **Pashupat Shaiv** sadhuon ki parampara ka bhi yahan gehra sambandh raha.

Aaj yeh parisar **Bharatiya Puratatva Sarvekshan (ASI)** ki dekhrekh mein sanrakshit hai.

## Kab jaayein?

- **Shravan maas** (July–August) mein yahan **Jageshwar Monsoon Mahotsav** hota hai.
- **Mahashivratri** par bada mela lagta hai.
- Saal bhar darshan hote hain, lekin sardiyon mein thand kaafi rehti hai.

> Devdar ki khushboo, Jataganga ki awaaz aur pattharon mein basa itihaas — Jageshwar mein bhakti aur virasat ek saath milti hain.
