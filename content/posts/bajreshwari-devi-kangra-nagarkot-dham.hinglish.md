Himachal ke **Kangra shehar** me door se hi ek mandir ka **sunehra kalash** chamakta dikhta hai. Pahad ke log ise pyaar se **"Nagarkot wali Mata"** ya **"Kangra Devi"** kehte hain. Ye hai **Maa Bajreshwari (Vajreshwari) Devi** ka dhaam — jise **Nagarkot Dham** bhi kaha jaata hai.

## Kahan hai Bajreshwari Devi mandir?

- **Sthaan:** Kangra shehar, zila Kangra, Himachal Pradesh
- **Purana naam:** Kangra ka purana naam **Nagarkot** tha, isliye mandir "Nagarkot Dham" kehlata hai
- **Paas me:** Aitihasik **Kangra Kila**; Kangra railway station se mandir lagbhag 2 kilometre
- **Devi:** Maa Durga ka **Vajreshwari** roop — "Vajra" yaani bijli/vajra aur "Ishwari" yaani Devi

## Sati ki katha — Shaktipeeth kyon?

Pauranik katha ke anusaar Raja Daksh ke yagya me Shiv ka apmaan hone par **Sati** ne yagya-kund me deh tyaag di. Shiv Sati ka shareer lekar brahmand me ghoomne lage, tab Bhagwan Vishnu ne **Sudarshan Chakra** se shareer ke ang alag kiye. Manyata hai ki is sthaan par **Sati ka baayan vakshasthal** gira tha — isliye ye **51 Shaktipeethon** me se ek hai.

Ek aur manyata hai ki Devi ne **vajra** se **Kalikaal** naam ke asur ka vadh kiya tha, isliye ve **Vajreshwari** kehlaayin.

## Makkhan wali pindi — Ghrit Mandal ki parampara

![Makkhan se dhaki Devi ki pindi aur dono or jalte diye (chitra)](/images/posts/bajreshwari-ghrit-mandal.jpg)
*Makar Sankranti par Maa ki pindi ko makkhan se dhaka jaata hai (chitra)*

Is mandir ki sabse anokhi parampara **Makar Sankranti** par hoti hai. Mandir me Devi **pindi roop** me viraajmaan hain. Lok manyata hai ki **Mahishasur** se yuddh ke baad Devi ke shareer par ghaav ho gaye the, aur unhe theek karne ke liye Devi ne Nagarkot me apne shareer par **makkhan** lagaya tha.

Isi yaad me Makar Sankranti par Maa ki pindi ko **makkhan (ghrit) se dhaka** jaata hai aur mandir me lagbhag **ek saptah** tak utsav chalta hai. Ise sthaaniya log **Ghrit Mandal** parv ke naam se jaante hain. Utsav ke baad ye makkhan prasad ke roop me baanta jaata hai.

## Dhyanu Bhagat ki kahani

Mukhya mandir ke saamne **Dhyanu Bhagat** ki moorti hai. Lok kathaon ke anusaar Dhyanu Bhagat Akbar ke samay ke Devi bhakt the, jinhone apni bhakti siddh karne ke liye Mata ko **apna sheesh** arpit kar diya tha. Unki katha Kangra aur Jwala Ji, dono jagah badi shraddha se sunayi jaati hai.

## Itihaas: Pandav, Ghaznavi aur 1905 ka bhookamp

- **Pandavon ki katha:** Manyata hai ki mool mandir **Pandavon** ne banwaya tha. Kehte hain ki Devi ne sapne me unhe Nagarkot me mandir banane ka aadesh diya tha.
- **Ghaznavi ka hamla:** Itihaas me darj hai ki **Mahmud Ghaznavi** ne Nagarkot par hamla kar mandir ko loota aur nasht kiya tha. Baad me mandir phir se bana.
- **1905 ka bhookamp:** **4 April 1905** ke vinaashkari **Kangra bhookamp** me ye mandir dhah gaya tha. Baad me iska **punarnirmaan** kiya gaya, aur aaj jo bhavya mandir dikhta hai wo usi ke baad ka hai.

Mandir parisar me **Bhairav** ka chhota mandir bhi hai, aur mukhya dwaar par **Nagarkhana** bana hai. Mandir kile ki tarah patthar ki deewar se ghira hai.

## Darshan ke liye zaroori baatein

- **Navratri** aur **Makar Sankranti** par yahan sabse zyada bheed hoti hai.
- Kangra Kila, aur nazdeek ke Jwala Ji va Chamunda Devi mandir bhi kai shraddhalu ek hi yatra me karte hain.
- Sardiyon me Dharamshala-Kangra kshetra me thand rehti hai — garam kapde saath rakhein.

Himachal ka doosra bada Shaktipeeth bhi padhein — [Naina Devi mandir, Bilaspur](/katha/naina-devi-mandir-bilaspur-shaktipeeth). Garhwal ka Shaktipeeth — [Surkanda Devi](/katha/surkanda-devi-shaktipeeth). Himachal ki sabhi kathayein [yahan](/kshetra/himachal) hain.

## Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

### Nagarkot Mata mandir kahan hai?
Nagarkot Mata yaani Bajreshwari Devi ka mandir Himachal Pradesh ke Kangra shehar me hai. Kangra ka purana naam Nagarkot tha, isliye ise Nagarkot Dham kehte hain.

### Bajreshwari Devi Shaktipeeth me Sati ka kaun-sa ang gira tha?
Manyata hai ki yahan Maa Sati ka baayan vakshasthal gira tha, isliye ye 51 Shaktipeethon me se ek hai.

### Kangra Devi ki pindi par makkhan kyon chadhaya jaata hai?
Manyata hai ki Mahishasur se yuddh ke baad Devi ke ghaavon par makkhan lagaya gaya tha. Isi yaad me Makar Sankranti par pindi ko makkhan se dhaka jaata hai aur lagbhag ek saptah utsav manaya jaata hai.

### Bajreshwari Devi mandir kab bana?
Manyata hai ki mool mandir Pandavon ne banwaya tha. Itihaas me kai baar is par hamle hue, aur 1905 ke Kangra bhookamp me dhahne ke baad iska punarnirmaan hua.

### Kangra railway station se mandir kitni door hai?
Kangra railway station se Bajreshwari Devi mandir lagbhag 2 kilometre door hai.
