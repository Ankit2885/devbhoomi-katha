Garhwal ke Rudraprayag zile mein, bugyalon aur buraansh ke jungle ke upar ek chhota-sa patthar ka mandir hai — **Tungnath**. Kareeb **3,680 metre** ki oonchai par bana yeh mandir **duniya ke sabse oonche Shiv mandiron** mein gina jaata hai aur **Panch Kedar mein sabse ooncha** hai.

"Tungnath" ka arth hai — **chotiyon ke swami**.

## Pandavon se judi katha

Mahabharat yuddh ke baad Pandav apne hi bhaiyon ki hatya ke paap se mukti ke liye Bhagwan Shiv ko khoj rahe the. Shiv unse bachne ke liye bail ka roop lekar Himalaya mein chhip gaye. Bheem ne bail ko pehchaan liya aur jab woh dharti mein samaane laga, to uske ang alag-alag jagah prakat hue.

Manyata hai ki **Tungnath mein Shiv ki bhujayein (baahu)** prakat huin. Baaki ang Kedarnath, Rudranath, Madhyamaheshwar aur Kalpeshwar mein pooje jaate hain — yahi hain **Panch Kedar**. (Poori katha hamari [Kedarnath aur Panch Kedar](/katha/kedarnath-aur-panch-kedar) wali katha mein padhein.)

## Ram aur Ravan ki manyatayein

Tungnath se lagbhag **2 kilometre upar** **Chandrashila** choti hai. Lok manyata ke anusaar Bhagwan **Ram** ne yahan dhyan kiya tha. Ek doosri manyata kehti hai ki **Ravan** ne bhi isi kshetra mein Shiv ki tapasya ki thi.

Chandrashila se Himalaya ki kai oonchi chotiyan ek saath dikhti hain, isliye yeh jagah yatriyon aur trekkers dono ki pasand hai.

## Yahan ke pujari alag kyon hain?

Kedarnath jaise kai dhamon mein pujari Dakshin Bharat se aate hain — yeh parampara Adi Shankaracharya se judi maani jaati hai. Lekin Tungnath mein pooja **Makkumath gaon ke sthaniya Maithani Brahmin** karte hain.

## Sardiyon mein Bhagwan kahan jaate hain?

Bhaari barfbaari ke kaaran Tungnath mandir saal mein **kareeb chhah mahine band** rehta hai. Is dauraan Bhagwan ki utsav murti aur pujari neeche **Makkumath** gaon ke **Markandeshwar Mandir** mein aa jaate hain, jo yahan se kareeb **29 kilometre** door hai. Sardiyon mein wahin darshan hote hain.

## Kaise pahunchein?

- Sabse nazdeeki sadak **Chopta** tak jaati hai.
- Chopta se Tungnath tak lagbhag **5 kilometre** ka paidal raasta hai.
- Wahan se Chandrashila kareeb 2 kilometre aur upar hai.
- Kapaat aamtaur par garmiyon mein khulte hain aur sardiyon se pehle band hote hain — yatra se pehle har saal ki tareekh zaroor dekh lein.

> Baadalon ke beech ghantiyon ki awaaz aur saamne Himalaya — Tungnath mein bhakti aur prakriti ek ho jaate hain.
