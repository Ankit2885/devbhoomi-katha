**Chamoli, Garhwal.** Bhagwan **Badri Vishal** ke dhaam **Badrinath** ke kapaat **Somvaar, 16 November 2026** ki raat **9 bajkar 37 minute** par vaidik mantrochchaar ke saath **sheetkaal ke liye band** kar diye gaye. Iske saath hi is saal ki **Chardham Yatra** poori ho gayi.

## Is saal record shraddhalu

Media reports ke anusaar is yatra season mein kareeb **23 lakh** shraddhaluon ne Badrinath Dham ke darshan kiye. Kapaat is baar lagbhag **207 din** khule rahe.

Kapaat band hone se pehle ke dinon mein dhaam mein **barfbaari** bhi hui, phir bhi aakhiri darshan ke liye badi sankhya mein bhakt pahunche.

## Kapaatbandi ki anokhi paramparayein

- **Rawal ka Sakhi roop:** Mandir ke mukhya pujari (**Rawal**) Amarnath Namboodiri ne parampara ke anusaar **Sakhi ka roop** dhaaran kiya aur **Mata Lakshmi** ko garbhgriha mein viraajmaan karaya. Manyata hai ki sheetkaal mein Mata Lakshmi Bhagwan Narayan ke saath garbhgriha mein rehti hain.
- **Ghrit Kambal:** Parampara hai ki kapaat band hone se pehle Bhagwan ko **ghee laga oonee kambal** odhaya jaata hai.
- **Panch Pooja:** Kapaatbandi se pehle kai dinon tak vishesh poojayein hoti hain, jinmein Maa Mahalakshmi ki vishesh pooja bhi shaamil hai.

## Ab kahan honge darshan?

Kapaat band hone ke baad agle lagbhag chhah mahine Bhagwan ki pooja **sheetkaleen gaddisthalon** par hoti hai:

- **Uddhav ji aur Kuber ji** ki doli **Pandukeshwar** jaati hai.
- **Adi Shankaracharya ki gaddi** **Jyotirmath (Joshimath)** pahunchti hai — reports ke anusaar yeh kareeb **43 kilometre** ki yatra hai.

## Chardham ke sheetkaleen gaddisthal

| Dhaam | Sheetkaal mein darshan |
|---|---|
| Badrinath | Pandukeshwar aur Jyotirmath |
| Kedarnath | Omkareshwar Mandir, Ukhimath |
| Gangotri | Mukhba gaon |
| Yamunotri | Kharsali gaon |

Sardiyon mein in jagahon ki **sheetkaleen yatra** bhi ki ja sakti hai. Kedarnath ke kapaat band hone ki khabar [yahan padhein](/katha/kedarnath-kapat-band-omkareshwar-2026).
